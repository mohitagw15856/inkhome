# Morning pages

The kettle clicks off and the flat goes quiet again. Three pages, no editing, no looking back: that is the whole rule.

Yesterday's draft ran long, so today I want something smaller. A letter to the version of me who started this, maybe, or a list of the small things that went right this week.

The light on the e-ink screen looks like paper, and that helps. Nothing pings. Nothing scrolls past. Just the cursor, waiting
