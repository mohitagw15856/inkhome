Inkhome

Install Inkhome on an Xteink X3 or X4 beside the stock firmware, and switch between the two. Nothing to download or build.

This browser cannot talk to the reader

Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer. Phones and Safari or Firefox do not support WebSerial.

1. Connect

  1. Connect the reader to this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Wake it (press Power), then press Connect straight away and choose the port (often USB JTAG/serial or usbmodem). If no port is listed, the reader has gone to sleep: press Power and try again.

Connect only reads the reader. Nothing is written until you press a button in step 2, so it is safe to connect just to look.

2. Choose

Is it safe? What if the reader sleeps or the cable comes out?
  • The reader cannot fall asleep while flashing. Once Connect succeeds, the reader's chip is held in its built-in loader, so neither the stock firmware nor Inkhome is running to put it to sleep. Installing takes about one to two minutes.
  • This page keeps your computer awake during an install. Keep the cable plugged in and the tab open until it says it is done.
  • If anything interrupts it (cable pulled, computer sleeps, tab closed), the reader still starts the firmware it had before. The page writes Inkhome into the spare slot, checks every byte with the chip, and only then switches which slot starts. Run Install again to finish.
  • If Inkhome ever misbehaves, connect here and press Start stock firmware. You can also switch from the reader: Apps, Switch firmware.
How it keeps stock safe

The reader has two firmware slots. The flasher reads both, identifies the stock firmware and only ever writes Inkhome into the other slot. Switching only rewrites the small record that says which slot starts; nothing is erased. Inkhome's own update options refuse to run while stock is in the other slot, so updates come through this page.

You can also switch from the reader: Apps, then Switch firmware.
