Inkhome

version __VERSION__ · Xteink X3 and X4

An X3-first build of CrossPoint Reader that also hosts the inkkit e-ink apps. One binary serves both the X3 and the X4; the device is detected when it starts.

Your browser cannot flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Flashing needs a secure (https) page.

  1. Connect the reader with a data USB-C cable and wake it.
  2. Press Connect and install and pick the serial port (often usbmodem on a Mac).
  3. Wait for the install to finish, then press the reset button or hold Power to restart.

Before you install. This writes a full image, including the partition table. It replaces the stock firmware and any second firmware slot. Take a full backup first if you may want stock back (for example esptool.py read_flash 0 0x1000000 backup.bin).

Already on Inkhome or CrossPoint? Update from the reader instead: copy inkhome-<version>-x3-x4.bin from the release page to the SD card and use Settings, then SD Card Firmware Update.
